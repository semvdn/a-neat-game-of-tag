# Initial gameplay and exhibition evaluation — 8 September 2026

## Scope

Fixed-policy mechanics tests, not retraining. No trained checkpoint was supplied. Four scripted Chaser/Runner pairs cover traversal/pursuit, camping, running without jumping, and a stationary Chaser. Six start classes cover visual, varied, close, normal, long, and midgame scenarios. Seeds are `0x51a7 + index × 7919`.

The final sweep used 32 seeds × 6 starts × 4 pairs × 3 conditions = **2,304 episodes**, each repeated with deep equality verification (**4,608 executions**). Encounter unit regressions also passed. The initial run took about 54 seconds on this machine; throughput is environment-dependent.

## Confirmed accounting defect

The old encounter code counted separation after falls as successful evasion even when it withheld the corresponding reward. It also checked only Runner falls when awarding the pressure bonus, allowing a Chaser fall to benefit its opponent indirectly. Distance exits and route exits duplicated this accounting.

`EncounterTracker` now cancels pressure on any registered fall and on tags. A later clean encounter can start afresh. Both distance and route exits use the same reward-cap calculation. This fixes a selection incentive and makes telemetry more trustworthy; it does not prove evolved behavior has improved yet.

Before/after comparison used 4 seeds × 6 starts × 4 pairs × 3 conditions = **288 paired episodes**. All recorded physical metrics matched exactly. In the baseline subset, successful evades fell from **0.490 to 0.365 per episode**. Mean pressure reward rose from **0.906 to 1.000** because fresh clean pressure after recovery is no longer disqualified by a stale pre-fall encounter. Fewer credited events therefore need not mean a lower total bonus.

Objective version advances to `clean-encounters-v10`; checkpoints and analysis exports identify the scoring change. The policy schema and shared physics remain unchanged.
